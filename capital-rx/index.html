What it does

The ask is a position, not a verdict.

Ask against band

The certified band is a percentile range of the domain's value distribution. The ask is marked on it. Because these distributions are right-skewed, mean-anchored comparables overstate the median asset by construction — which is exactly the argument your counterparty will make in reverse.

  • No certificate means no relation — the interface shows an em dash and never a default
  • Conditions are enumerated and travel with the band
