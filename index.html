Inside an assessment record

Seven parts. Twenty-five sections. Every gap stated.

Every assessment record follows a ratified architecture, and every statement in one is classified by what stands behind it. Four conventions govern the whole document; the full part-by-part architecture ships in the specimen, not here.

Certificate record

Reproduced exactly as recorded from a signed source. Never paraphrased, never improved on.

Computed

Arithmetic over enumerated inputs, recomputable by anyone holding the same record. The computation is named.

Analytical

Synthesis, attributed to its method and signed as part of the record. Analysis is never presented as record.

Stated absence

Where the record supports nothing, the section says so and stays empty. A gap is rendered, never filled.
