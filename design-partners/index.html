The Foster Rx Founding Design-Partner Program

Three organizations. Three roles. One bounded window. The program ends Q1 2027.

Foster Rx builds certified assessments for life-sciences assets — independently verifiable analysis for the moments when analysis must survive hostile reading. Every certificate carries deterministic, cited computation; the signature of a named, credentialed human reviewer; and a public verification link any counterparty can check, free, forever. The system refuses to certify when review is absent — enforced in code.

Before public launch, we are working with exactly three design partners, each holding a different evidentiary role. This page is the whole program.

The three roles

  1. Transmission-native advisory intermediary in contracting

    An advisory firm whose daily work is sending analysis into adversarial readings on live transactions.

  2. University technology transfer open

    A large R1 research university’s licensing or innovation-foundation team, whose signed valuations travel into negotiations with counterparties who structurally discount what they cannot verify.

  3. Audit-exposed institution open

    An organization whose analysis is read by a regulator, auditor, or oversight body as a matter of obligation — the reader who must be adversarial.

What design partners receive

  • Early-access windows on a defined clock — timing is the benefit, never price; our rate card is public, versioned, and uniform.
  • Direct founder-level access and standing methodology input.
  • Aggregate verification instrumentation on your certificates, with co-marketing rights to the figures.
  • Founding-cohort naming at launch, by mutual written consent.

What we ask

  • A live transaction entering diligence within two quarters — calendared, not hypothetical.
  • A named template: the certificate and verification link in your actual client-facing deliverable, in writing.
  • Story rights, de-identified where needed.
  • An adversarial reader on the other side.

What disqualifies — stated plainly

  • No live transaction in the window.
  • A brand so strong the certificate has no work to do.
  • Unwillingness to name the template.

We would rather leave a slot open than fill it with a partner whose deals can’t generate evidence.

The covenants we publish

  • Verification is free, forever.
  • The program’s rules, our refusal criteria, and our transparency schema are public and versioned.
  • We publish our numbers when they are zeros.

The clock

The founding cohort closes when the two open slots fill or on October 31, 2026, whichever comes first. The program sunsets Q1 2027; alumni convert at the public rate card. There is no second cohort unless the first one’s evidence justifies it — and we will publish that evidence either way.
