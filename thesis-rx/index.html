What it does

Four things, and it refuses to guess at any of them.

Triage on decision clocks

Fee windows, prosecution responses, national phase entries, reassessments blocked on a grant. Sorted by date, never by a score, and every row carries its coverage so triage never happens against an unknown evidence base.

Events that resolve

An inter partes review is filed. A citation lands. An assignment records. Each one resolves to a signed change or a logged dismissal — and a dismissal stays on the feed, greyed and labelled, because a feed that quietly drops what it decided was unimportant cannot be audited by its reader.

Valuation, signed

A range against the derived value distribution for the domain, with conditions enumerated. Never a point estimate, and never a figure without the conditions that hold it up.

Patent and claim analysis

Deterministic claim charts with character-anchored citations — every assertion traceable to a character range in the source document. Every analysis is attributed and states its scope, date and jurisdiction. It draws no legal conclusions and is not an attorney-signed opinion.
