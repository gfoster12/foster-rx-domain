Cookie policy

What this website stores in your browser, why, and how to refuse it.

Effective 23 August 2026 · Foster Rx Systems Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Annapolis, Maryland (“Foster Rx”, “we”, “us”)

The short version

This site sets no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, and no cross-site trackers. The certificate verification page sets nothing that identifies you.

What is stored

Appearance preference. If you switch between light and dark appearance, we store that choice in your browser's local storage under the key frx-theme so the site remembers it. It contains the word “light” or “dark” and nothing else. It is never sent to a server.

Fonts. Typefaces are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to a Google server that includes your IP address. No cookie is set by us for this purpose.

Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs, described in the privacy policy.

How to refuse or remove it

Clear your browser's site data for this domain, or use private browsing. Removing the appearance preference has no effect other than resetting the site to follow your operating system's setting. No functionality is lost.

If this changes

If we ever introduce analytics or any cookie that is not strictly necessary, this page will be updated first and consent will be requested before anything is set.
