Trust & security

What we run on, and what we commit to.

Written for the procurement team, not the marketing page. Where a control is not yet in place, this page says so.

Infrastructure

Where the platform runs

Hosting

Google Cloud Platform, region us-east4. Compute on Cloud Run; application data in Firestore.

Business associate agreement

A HIPAA business associate agreement is in place with the cloud provider covering the production project.

Signing

Certificates are signed with Ed25519. The public trust anchor is published at a stable address and verification resolves against it directly.

Verification path

The verification page performs a client-side read against a published projection. No Foster Rx server sits in the verification path, which is what allows verification to remain free and permanent.

Audit substrate

Operational events are recorded to a hash-chained append-only log. Agent build sessions are recorded to a separate chained ledger with per-session attribution.

Access

Internal and demonstration surfaces sit behind identity-aware access control with an explicit principal allowlist. Production surfaces are not publicly invocable.

Data handling

What we hold, and what we do not

Two layers, and no vendor feed in the certified corpus

Certified claims derive from primary registry records ingested from issuing authorities, and from Foster Rx internal methodologies. No commercial data feed sits behind a certified claim.

Client material stays yours

Documents supplied for an engagement are used for that engagement. Retention and any secondary use are set in the services agreement, in writing, before work begins.

Assessments are recorded

Every assessment we produce is recorded whether or not it is transmitted, so calibration is measured over everything rather than only over favourable results. Recording is internal; publication is your decision.

Corrections are signed, not deleted

If an assessment is wrong, a superseding certificate is issued and the original stays verifiable. Nothing is quietly withdrawn.

Certifications

Stated plainly

Foster Rx does not currently hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestation. The controls above are in place and documented; the third-party attestation is not yet complete, and claiming otherwise on a website would be exactly the behaviour this company exists to argue against. Enterprise buyers who require an attestation before contracting should raise it early so the timeline is honest on both sides.

Documented policies. Information security policy, incident response plan, editorial content review procedure and funds transfer verification procedure are maintained and available under NDA on request.
